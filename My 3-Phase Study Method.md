**① Read → ② Practice → ③ Watch → ④ Repeat**

→ **Phase 1 — Read the Notes 📚**  
I made my notes based on **AWS official documentation**.  
You don't need to understand everything immediately. Just read through it and get familiar with the concepts.

→ **Phase 2 — Practice 📝**  
Then, I jumped into practice questions using the **free resources here**.  
This will challenge your mind to remember concepts/terms and how the questions will just confuse you with its wordings/structure.

→ **Phase 3 — Watch 🎥**  
Only after getting familiar with the concepts did I watch videos. You will not be bored, I promise since you already know the concepts, it will be more like **someone telling a story about something you already knew.**  
At this point, it didn't feel like learning everything from scratch anymore. This is where I learned what inference really is, as it can be confusing with Fine-Tuning and RAG.
